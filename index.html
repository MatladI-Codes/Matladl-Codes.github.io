CHAPTER ONE: THE BUILDER

I didn't come from Silicon Valley. I came from Katlehong — a township in the south of Johannesburg that the world rarely puts on a map. No trust fund. No laptop. No blueprint. Just a dying phone, an internet connection, and an obsessive need to build things that matter. I taught myself to code through Harvard's CS50 and IBM's AI curriculum — not because someone told me to, but because waiting was never an option.

I left North-West University not as someone who failed, but as someone who found the wrong map. Biochemistry sharpened my precision. The pivot sharpened my purpose. Today I operate SEDIQ. — a holding concept and pilot in progress, housing ventures in AI, ParentTech, and beyond. I'm twenty years old, based in Johannesburg, and actively exploring prompt engineering with Claude (Anthropic), ChatGPT (OpenAI), Replit Agent, and Lovable Agent. I'm just getting started.
